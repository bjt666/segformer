# UnravelNet reference

The F2 adapter in `segformer_baseline/unravel.py` adapts the channel-group,
directional-context and quality-feature interaction design of:

Wei Lu et al., **UnravelNet: A backbone for enhanced multi-scale and low-quality
feature extraction in remote sensing object detection**, ISPRS Journal of
Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing 231 (2026), 431–442.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2025.11.002

Author code: https://github.com/AeroVILab-AHU/LEGNet/blob/main/mmrotate/models/backbones/unravelnet.py
